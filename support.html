Support: Fair Deal Pinochle

Something wrong, or a question? Write to okant@prondi.com. There is no account and no form: just an e-mail. Please include your phone model and the app version.

Common questions

  • How do I get "Remove ads" back on a new phone? Open Settings and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.
  • Why do I see ads? The free version shows ads on menu screens and one full-screen ad at the end of a game (not after your first finished game). There are no ads between hands or while you play a hand. "Remove ads" is a one-time purchase that removes all of them.
  • Can I resume a game I closed? Yes. Your game is saved. Continue it from Home.
  • Does the app need an account or internet? No account. The game works offline; only ads, the consent form and purchases use the internet.

Report an ad

Ads appear only after a game and on menu screens. If an ad does not belong in a game of cards, or you think it is unsafe, write to the address above and tell us when and where you saw it and who the advertiser was, if the ad shows it.
